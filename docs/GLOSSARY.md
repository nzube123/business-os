# Glossary

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Business OS | The multi-tenant operating system for running a business workflow digitally |
| Tenant | An individual business workspace that owns its own records and users |
| RBAC | Role-based access control, used as a grouping mechanism for permissions |
| Permission | A discrete authorization right such as customer.read or invoice.create |
| AI Assistant | A governed subsystem that helps users interact with business data through validated tools |
| Inventory Movement | A record of a stock increase or decrease |
| Audit Event | A recorded action that captures who changed what and when |
| Business Service | A domain service responsible for enforcing business rules and workflows |
| Tool | A validated function that the AI assistant can invoke to perform an action safely |
| Customer Portal | A self-service experience for customers to interact with businesses |
